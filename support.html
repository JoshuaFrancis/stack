STACK — Block Stacking Arcade Game

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my progress on a new device?

STACK automatically syncs your high score, coins, and unlocked themes through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on both devices and that iCloud is enabled for STACK in your device settings.

My lives aren't regenerating. What should I do?

Lives regenerate one every 30 minutes, up to a maximum of 5. Make sure your device's date and time are set to automatic (Settings > General > Date & Time > Set Automatically). If the clock has been manually changed, the game may pause regeneration as a precaution.

How do I unlock new themes?

Earn coins by playing games, claiming daily rewards, spinning the lucky wheel, or watching rewarded ads. Visit the Shop to browse and purchase themes with your coins.

Ads aren't loading. Is something wrong?

Ad availability depends on your region and network connection. If no ads are available, try again later. You can still earn coins through gameplay and daily rewards.

How do I connect to Game Center?

STACK connects to Game Center automatically if you're signed in on your device. Check Settings > Game Center to verify your sign-in status. Leaderboard scores and achievements will sync once connected.

Can I turn off sound or haptics?

Yes. Tap the gear icon on the main menu to access Settings, where you can toggle sound effects, music, and haptic feedback independently.
