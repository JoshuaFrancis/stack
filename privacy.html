Privacy Policy

STACK — Last updated March 12, 2026

Overview

STACK is a block-stacking arcade game developed by Joshua Francis. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Collect

STACK itself does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. The following data may be collected through third-party services:

Advertising

STACK displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA), subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice
  • General device information (device model, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps)

This data is used to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt or adjusting your device settings under Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Apple may collect data related to leaderboards and achievements in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. STACK does not access your Apple ID or personal Game Center profile information beyond your public player alias.

iCloud

STACK uses Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store to sync your game progress (high score, coins, unlocked themes) across your devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

Local Data

Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard system storage. This data never leaves your device except through iCloud sync as described above.

Children's Privacy

STACK is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require account creation, and ad personalization can be disabled at the system level.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Data collected by AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy and data practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at hello@joshuafrancis.ca.
